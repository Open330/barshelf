# BarShelf 0.7.26

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

## Changes

- Long lists no longer make a card endlessly tall. A searchable list, or one with more than 12 rows, shows about ten rows and scrolls the rest, with its search field kept above them. The Bitwarden card with a full vault was taller than the screen. Widget authors can set the list's `height`.
