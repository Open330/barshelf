# BarShelf 0.3.12

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon.

## Fixes

- **Menu bar items stay where you put them.** Each item now has its own
  name, so macOS remembers an arrangement made with ⌘-drag across launches
  and updates. Before, only the BarShelf icon kept its place, and an item
  could reappear on the far side of other apps' icons.
