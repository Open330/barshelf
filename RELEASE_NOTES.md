# BarShelf 0.7.9

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

## Changes

- **Keep Awake.** A new widget keeps the Mac awake for 1, 2 or 4 hours or
  until a time you choose — never indefinitely, and never longer than 12
  hours — and lists whatever else is keeping it awake. In the menu bar it
  shows the time left. It works with PowerDisplayGuard, which owns the power
  settings; Keep Awake only asks, and says plainly when the guard isn't
  answering.
