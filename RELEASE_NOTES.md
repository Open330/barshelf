# BarShelf 0.7.11

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

## Changes

- **A calmer, more native popup.** The popup is now one sheet of the
  system's popover material — Liquid Glass on macOS 26 — with each widget
  on its own soft card instead of rows split by hairlines. A card's header
  shows the widget's symbol on a small tile of its colour and its name;
  the update time steps back. Big readings (a percentage, a temperature,
  time left) are drawn in one rounded face with steady digits, and spacing,
  corners and colours now come from one set of design tokens throughout the
  popup and widget content.
