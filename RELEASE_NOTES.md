# BarShelf 0.7.14

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

## Changes

- **Menu bar cluster alert dot.** Folded chips now lie left over right, so
  each chip's red dot sits on that chip instead of on the neighbour that
  covers it. The dot scales with its chip as it grows into a pill.
- **Keep Awake card** leads with its own state and puts Stop on its own row,
  instead of repeating the menu bar reading above it.
- **Menu bar cards** can grow to 560 points before their content scrolls.
- **Landing page** shows the menu bar and the popup in motion, recorded from
  the app's own views.
