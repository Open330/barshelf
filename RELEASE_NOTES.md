# BarShelf 0.7.44

> Unreleased. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.

## Changes

- Chips in the menu bar are stacked again by default, each one over the next, as before 0.7.43. Side by side is still there under Settings ▸ Menu Bar ▸ Chips ▸ Spacing; if you chose a spacing there, it is kept.
- A stacked chip's icon stays in the middle of its chip at full size. In 0.7.43 it was made smaller and moved into the part of the chip left in view, so the icons no longer lined up with their chips.
